Four things that decide

Every market we look at gets scored on the same four axes. No exceptions.

🎓
#1 Priority
School District
Two young kids in school means this is our absolute top filter. A great school district doesn't just improve education — it protects long-term home value. No compromises here.
2
🚆
#2 Priority
Commute to Midtown
Thirty minutes of commute difference adds up to 125 extra hours a year. We track door-to-door time via LIRR, Metro-North, and NJ Transit — not just train schedules.

#3 Priority
Monthly PITI
The real number — Principal, Interest, Taxes, and Insurance combined. Our ceiling is $5,000/month. The dashboard recalculates this automatically every time rates move.

🏷️

Our sweet spot is $700K–$1M. Above $832K, we cross into jumbo loan territory and pay a rate premium. So price matters — but it's the last filter, not the first.
