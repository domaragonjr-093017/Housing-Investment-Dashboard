## Hoboken, NJ

### Home Prices
- Median home value: **~$872,100**
- Primarily condos/townhomes; limited single-family inventory

### Property Taxes
- Effective rate: **~1.14%** (36% below Hudson County avg of 1.77%)
- Estimated annual bill: **~$9,948**
- **Major 2026–27 increases incoming:**
  - School budget: +23.6% tax hike ($104.8M budget approved)
  - Municipal taxes: +~18.9%
  - Combined overall increase: ~18%
- Source: [Hudson County View](https://hudsoncountyview.com/hoboken-boe-unanimously-approves-tentative-104-8m-budget-with-27-tax-hike/), [Hoboken Girl](https://www.hobokengirl.com/hoboken-jersey-city-property-tax-increase-2026/)

### Schools
- **#1 school district in Hudson County** (Niche)
- GreatSchools avg: **~8/10**
- Math proficiency: 58% (vs. 38% NJ avg); Reading: 67% (vs. 49% NJ avg)
- Notable schools:
  - Wallace Elementary: 8/10
  - Hoboken Middle School: 7/10
  - Hoboken High School: 6/10
  - Dr. Ronald McNair High School: 10/10
- Source: [GreatSchools](https://www.greatschools.org/new-jersey/hoboken/), [Jill Biggs Group](https://www.thejillbiggsgroup.com/blog/hoboken-public-schools-the-ultimate-guide-for-families)

## Montclair, NJ

### Home Prices
- Median sale price: **~$1,000,000+** (exceeded $1M avg monthly median; up ~50% in 5 years)
- Strong demand driven by top schools and NYC commuter access

### Property Taxes
- Effective rate: **~3.38%** (higher than NJ state median of 2.88%)
- Average annual bill: **~$22,487** (on assessed value ~$639,628)
- 2026–27 school levy increase: +3.9% (~$851 more for median-value home)
- Rates vary by ZIP: $16,682 (07042) to $23,065 (07043)
- Source: [Montclair Local](https://montclairlocal.news/2026/03/with-no-official-tax-rate-for-26-27-budget-residents-make-their-own-estimates/), [Patch](https://patch.com/new-jersey/montclair/average-property-tax-tops-22k-montclair-see-latest-5-year-breakdown), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-01-09/montclair-new-jersey-home-affordability-to-face-test-with-march-tax-hike-vote)

### Schools
- District Niche grade: **A**
- Montclair High School: Niche A, #102 Best Public HS in NJ, GreatSchools 6/10
- Ridgewood Avenue Elementary: GreatSchools 9/10
- Math proficiency: 55% (vs. 38% NJ avg); Reading: 69% (vs. 49% NJ avg)
- 11 schools, ~6,278 students, student-teacher ratio 15:1
- Source: [Niche](https://www.niche.com/k12/d/montclair-public-schools-nj/), [GreatSchools](https://www.greatschools.org/new-jersey/montclair/)

## Ridgewood, NJ

### Home Prices
- Median home price: **~$1,050,000–$1,349,000** (April 2026 median: $1,349,000)
- Range: $800,000–$1.6M depending on school zone/location

### Property Taxes
- Effective rate: **~2.73–2.80%** (near NJ state median of 2.88%)
- Median annual bill: **~$16,952–$21,000**
- Property taxes driven significantly by highly funded school district
- Source: [Ownwell](https://www.ownwell.com/trends/new-jersey/bergen-county/ridgewood), [Bergen Real Estate](https://bergenrealestate.com/ridgewood-nj-homes-apartments-for-sale-rent-property-tax/)

### Schools
- GreatSchools average: **9/10** (9 public schools)
- Widely regarded as one of Bergen County's top districts
- Award-winning schools with vibrant community programs
- Source: [Home Navigators](https://maryanneelsaesserhomenavigators.com/blog/understanding-ridgewood-nj-property-tax-system-for-home-buyers)

## White Plains, NY

### Home Prices
- Average home value: **~$694,605** (up 3.2% year-over-year)
- Homes go to pending in ~35 days
- Source: [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/34819/white-plains-ny/)

### Property Taxes
- Effective rate: **~1.97%** (above NY state median of 1.90%)
- Median annual bill: **~$9,854** (on median assessed value of $526,300)
- Range by ZIP: $4,515 (10601) to $11,996 (10605)
- Source: [Ownwell](https://www.ownwell.com/trends/new-york/westchester-county/white-plains), [LevyIO](https://levyio.com/taxes/white-plains-ny/)

### Schools
- White Plains City School District serves a large, diverse student population
- Detailed 2026 ratings available on [Niche](https://www.niche.com/k12/d/white-plains-city-school-district-ny/) and [GreatSchools](https://www.greatschools.org/new-york/white-plains/)
- Generally considered average to above-average for Westchester County
- Note: District serves a broader socioeconomic mix than other towns on this list

## Garden City, NY

### Home Prices
- Typical home value: **~$1,071,019** (up 1.4% year-over-year)
- Source: [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/31695/garden-city-ny/)

### Property Taxes
- Effective rate: **~1.46%** (below NY state median of 2.39%)
- Median annual bill: **~$12,269**
- Source: [Ownwell](https://www.ownwell.com/trends/new-york/nassau-county/garden-city)

### Schools
- GreatSchools average: **10/10** — top 5% of New York public schools
- Math proficiency: **88%** (vs. 52% NY avg); Reading: **76%** (vs. 49% NY avg)
- Garden City High School: Niche A+, student-teacher ratio 11:1
- Garden City Middle School: Niche A, GreatSchools 8/10, student-teacher ratio 12:1
- Source: [GreatSchools](https://www.greatschools.org/best-schools/new-york/garden-city/?st[]=public), [Niche](https://www.niche.com/k12/garden-city-high-school-garden-city-ny/)
